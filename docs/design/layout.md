# Layout

- A bird that appears goes to its saved (dragged) position if it has one, otherwise to a free cell of a grid sized from the garden and one bird's footprint, starting from a cell picked by its id. A watch block that forms goes to the spot that moves its birds the least on average. Its birds keep their cells afterwards (while a cell is free and next to a bird it is linked to), so a bird that joins later doesn't move the others; when the block grows to the left or up, the block's corner moves so the birds already there stay put (Claude's own decision)
- Each bird and block takes a footprint: the most room it can need (name, icon, bubble room, both status lines, marks, and for a watching bird the count row). Every row of a watch block has that fixed height too (name row, bubble room, count row), so a bubble, a name inside the block, or the count appearing never moves a bird in it (Claude's own decision; the empty name row of a block is no longer tightened). A block is as wide and tall as its cells need ("Watching"); when it doesn't fit, the window grows like for any bird. Neighbours keep a 12px gap between footprints, so bubbles and status lines coming and going never make them overlap. A bird's footprint is a bird's width (120px, which holds the name and status lines), not the widest bubble's (180px) (the user's approval: 「なおして」). The footprint had been widened to 180px (d74467f) so that a bubble never reached a neighbour's status line (a UI check found a bubble 8px over the next bird's status line with a 120px footprint); that left the birds sparse (the user, on the gallery's first image and the download page: 「てかかんかくひろがりすぎじゃない？なんでこんなにすかすかなんん」, and on the cause: 「そうやな、ちょうどアプリもちょっとすかすかなきはしてた。なおして」)
  - A bubble shows its whole text, never cut with "…", and is drawn above every bird, status line and mark, so it may cover a neighbour's icon, status lines or bubble; bubbles from later turns are drawn on top (the user: 「せりふはしょうりゃくせずにうえにかさねる」「いいから、やれ」). A bird the user dragged comes in front instead: while dragged and after it is dropped, that bird with its bubble, status lines and marks is drawn above every other bird and bubble, the last dragged frontmost (the user: 「zindexは、ドラッグしたやつゆうせんにして」). The name and status lines of a bird in front get a backing in the garden's background color, so they stay readable over a bubble; over the plain garden it doesn't show (the user, asked with a screenshot where a dragged bird's grey name over a cream bubble couldn't be read: 「つける」). The order is saved with the positions, so overlaps look the same after a relaunch; birds never dragged keep the order above (Claude's own decision). Each bubble (its tail included) casts a drop shadow, so overlapping bubbles stay apart to the eye (the user: 「せりふがかさなったときにくべつしっやすいよう、かげつけてな」). It is as wide as its text up to its max width and wraps onto more lines beyond that, centered under its bird with the tail right under the icon, shifted only to stay inside the garden. The max width is 180px, narrowed so it overlaps the bubbles beside it as little as possible, but never below 120px (a bird's width) (the user: 「せりふやが、minwdithをしていしたうえで、できるだけかさならないようにすることはできる？」, approving the proposal with 「おk」): for each bird with a bubble outside a watch block, take the birds with a bubble next to it on the left and right whose bubbles overlap its own vertically, judged by each bubble's whole height after wrapping (every line, not only the first) and wherever it is shown (below or above its bird), birds inside a watch block and the concierge included (the user: 「かさなりはんていを、たぶんいちぎょうめけしかやってないやろそれじゃあかん」; before, only birds whose top edges were within 12px counted, so a wrapped bubble reaching the next row was never checked); the bubble may reach at most halfway between its bird's center and that neighbour's center on that side, less 4px, so neighbouring bubbles keep an 8px gap and don't read as one block (Claude's own decision, after a UI check found bubbles touching at 0px looked joined), and the max width is twice the nearer of the two half-distances, kept between 120px and 180px. A bubble shifted to stay inside the garden keeps the halfway limit on the other side too: the max width is also at most the room to the garden edge on one side plus the half-distance to the neighbour on the other, so a bubble near the edge gets narrower and wraps onto more lines instead of reaching over its neighbour (Claude's own decision: a bubble shifted 2px at the edge overlapped the next one by 2px, and 26px for a bird 40px from the edge). With no neighbour with a bubble on either side it stays 180px. Birds placed side by side are 132px apart (120px footprint + 12px gap), so their bubbles at 120px don't overlap. The 120px floor keeps bubbles from growing many lines tall (Claude's own decision). When birds stand closer than that (a bubble at the floor would still reach over its neighbour), the bubbles that overlap vertically are moved sideways apart instead of narrowing further: laid out left to right, each keeps the 8px gap to the one before it and stays inside the garden, and each may move off its bird's center by at most its half-width less 12px, so the tail stays right under the icon and on the bubble's body, not on its rounded corner. Bubbles of birds inside a watch block move the same way. A pair that can't be moved apart within those limits doesn't pass its push on to a third bubble when that would make a new overlap there (Claude's own decision: two bubbles stacked at the same x pushed one of them 48px into its left neighbour, overlapping it by 22px where it had a 26px gap). When that isn't enough, a bubble of the pair is shown above its bird instead, above its name so the name stays readable (tail pointing down past the name at the icon) if it fits there without overlapping another bubble, the one whose move clears more overlap first. When it would fit above but for the garden's top edge, the window grows upward until it fits, the same way it grows downward for a bubble past the bottom edge. It grows upward for this even when the window sits in the top part of the display, where it otherwise keeps its top edge, as the drag growth does toward the edge being pushed; only the growth for the bubble goes up, the rest follows the thirds as before (the user chose 「そのときだけ上へ伸ばす」; Claude's own decision to grow upward at all, following the user's choice of growing the window: at the user's 16:08 layout the bubble placed above its name passed the top edge by about 8px, leaving a 53px overlap). Only when neither works (birds less than about 28px apart with no room above, or squeezed against the garden edge) do they still overlap, as in the rule above (Claude's own decision, after the user saw two bubbles 100px apart overlap by 20px, and three bubbles crowded at the user's 16:08 layout still overlapped by 53px after moving sideways: 「かさなりもかいぜんしてないで。」). A bubble that would reach past the garden's bottom edge, where the garden cuts it off, is shown above its bird instead, above its name, with the tail pointing down at the icon (Claude's own decision: a bubble shown right above the icon hid its bird's name, and the user-reviewer couldn't tell whose question it was), so its whole text stays visible; it goes above only when it fits there without overlapping another bubble after moving sideways; otherwise it stays below and the window grows downward until the bubble fits inside the garden, like the growth for fit (up to the visible area of the display), so it is neither cut nor hidden. The grown size is automatic growth, shrinking back only by the common shrink rule (birds leave or a drop, never moving anyone) (the user chose 「窓を下へ伸ばす」, asked with a screenshot where a bubble shown above to avoid a 1px cut was hidden 54px under the bubble right above it). At the display's limit it goes on the side where less of it is cut or covered (Claude's own decision). Since a bubble's height depends on its width, the widths, positions and heights are worked out again until they no longer change (a few rounds at most); a bubble wrapped tall enough to reach the next row is checked against that row's bubbles the same way. Before, each bubble was kept short of its neighbours' icons, marks, status lines and bubbles and cut with "…" to fit (8561685, parent session's spec), which cut most bubbles once birds stood close
  - At rest (Fade until hovered) the status lines are hidden. A bird's own status lines go below one line of bubble room; a bubble wrapped onto more lines covers them, like any other overlap (Claude's own decision: pushing the status lines down by the bubble's height took them off their bird into the next row, a 4-line bubble moving a row's status lines 44px down onto the next row's names). A bird in the same row as a bird with a bubble (top edges within 12px) keeps the bubble room too, the same as in a watch block's row, so the status lines of a row line up. The footprint already has that room, so nothing else moves (Claude's own decision)
  - Inside a watch block the cells are a bird's width too. A bubble there shows its whole text and is drawn on top too, but is at most a bird's width (120px) and wraps onto more lines, so the bubbles of birds side by side in a block don't overlap; a block's birds can't be moved apart one by one (the user, on a screenshot of two neighbours' bubbles overlapping in a block: 「グループ固定位置でセリフがかぶるのはちょっときになるなぁ」, approving this with 「うん」)
- A bird or watch block on screen stays at the same place on screen until the user drags it (the user asked that birds stop moving around). Nothing else moves it: bubbles appearing or disappearing, tool / status lines, text wrapping, watch links forming or changing, other birds joining or leaving, and the window growing or shrinking. The only exception is a bird that would otherwise overlap another bird or block; then only that one moves, to the nearest free spot. A bird the user dropped over another is not such a bird (below)
  - "On screen" means shown in a settled place: a bird or block first placed in a render that had to overlap it because there was no spot (the render that asks the window to grow) doesn't count as on screen yet. After the window grows it is placed again like a bird that just appeared, and the growth is estimated the same way. Birds that were already settled don't move. Before, birds first placed at launch in the small garden were kept at those places after the window grew, which split the free space so that a block and three birds overlapped at 634×726 while placing them afresh fit at 632×723 (Claude's own decision; the user left the Garden overlap to Claude: 「まかせる」)
    - Birds that were already settled are not placed again even when the window has reached the display and a block formed among them later still can't be placed; they overlap, as in the general rule (the user, asked whether to place every bird again only in that case: 「そんなばめんそうそうないし、そこにめんてなんすこすとかけられない」)
  - Positions are kept in px, not as a share of the garden. When the window is resized (grown, shrunk, or by hand), they are shifted by how far the garden's corner moved on screen, so they stay at the same place on screen. A bird or block that then sticks out of the garden also moves to the nearest free spot inside
  - A bird the user drops stays where it was dropped, even over another bird or a watch block; it is not nudged, and nothing else moves for it. They overlap (the user, on the nudge: 「おしださなくていいでしょ」「ユーザーはそこにおきたくておいてるんだから」, and 「macのですくとっぷあいこんもすきなところにおけるよね」, approving overlap with 「おk」). It is kept inside the garden like every bird, and keeps that spot, overlap included, after a relaunch. Before, a bird dropped onto another was nudged next to it (Claude's own decision), which put it somewhere the user didn't drop it
    - A watch block the user drops is treated the same: it stays where it was dropped, its birds keep their cells in it (Claude's own decision, following the user's words)
    - A bird or block the user placed this way is marked as placed by the user, on screen and in its saved position, until it is dragged again or leaves. An overlap with it is not "can't be placed": the window doesn't grow for it, the icon size and the grow/shrink estimates count it as placed, and later birds still avoid its footprint. Saved positions without the mark are placed as before, so saved spots that overlap after the garden changed size still come apart (Claude's own decision)
  - Which one moves when two would overlap otherwise: blocks already on screen come first, then birds already on screen (the earlier shown first), then what just appeared (Claude's own decision)
    - What moves or can't have the spot it wants goes to the nearest free spot whose top is level with a bird or block already placed, so it lines up with a row; only when there is no such spot does it go to the nearest free spot at any height (Claude's own decision, issue #3)
  - Positions are saved as each bird's center and top edge in % of the garden, and are used only when a bird appears (e.g. after a relaunch). On screen, birds are anchored at their top edge, so a line added under a bird doesn't move its icon
  - What is saved after a drop is where every bird settled on screen, not where the pointer was released, so the Garden opens after a relaunch as it was right after the drop; birds in a block are saved at their spot in it, and saved spots of birds that are gone are pruned. Before, only the dropped bird was saved, at the pointer: a bird dropped onto another settled beside it but came back at the pointer after a reload, and birds not dragged took other grid cells, since all birds appearing at once are placed by id (a UI check: `bubble-520-B-hover.png` / `bubble-520-B-reload.png`) (Claude's own decision)
- Right-clicking the Garden opens a menu with "Arrange" (ja: 整列) (issue #4; the user asked for a right-click menu 「整列とか色々そういうのをつけたい」 and chose only "Arrange" from "Arrange", "open the bird's terminal / editor", and "put the bird back"). The menu is the same on a bird, a watch block, and an empty spot (Claude's own decision: with one item there is nothing to change by place)
  - Arrange drops every saved (dragged) position and every "placed by the user" mark, and lays out all birds and watch blocks again as on a launch with no saved positions. It is the one other thing, besides the user's drag, that moves birds on screen. The result is saved like a drop, and the window then shrinks once by the same rule as after a drop (Claude's own decision)
    - The icon size is worked out again for the garden's current size, as on a launch. When the birds don't all fit, they are placed like birds that appear: the window grows for them, they are placed again at the grown size, and only once every bird has a spot is the result saved (with no bird marked as placed by the user and no front order) and the window shrinks once. Until then nothing is saved but the cleared positions, so a relaunch in between lays out as with no saved positions. A drop in between ends the Arrange and is saved as a drop (Claude's own decision)
  - The menu is the system's own menu popped up at the cursor (Tauri's native menu), not one drawn in the page: at rest the window is fully transparent and takes clicks only over birds, the handle, and the concierge's speech bubble, so a menu drawn in the page would not show or take clicks there. It replaces the WebView's own menu (reload etc.) in the Garden. At rest, a right-click on a bird opens it; on an empty spot the click goes through to the window below, like any click there. Its text follows the UI language (Claude's own decision)
  - Mock mode in a browser has no native menu; the mock controls have "Arrange the Garden" for checking the result (Claude's own decision)
- The Garden never sticks out of the window and never scrolls, in any state. When birds can't all fit, they overlap instead (the user's decision: 「ウィンドウからはみだすのはあかんわやっぱり。スクロール状態になるのがあかん。それよりはあいこんがかさなるほうがまだまし」)
- Every bird whose process is alive is placed in the Garden, however long it has been idle, including `dozing` birds. There is no nest
- Birds are not made any smaller to fit: the icon's lower limit (26px) is the smallest that stays readable (the user's decision)
- The icon size follows the garden size and the number of birds, but it grows only when birds leave and nothing on screen would have to move for it, never when the window grows: a larger icon makes every bird taller and would push neighbours away (Claude's own decision)
- When the birds don't fit in the floating window's Garden tab, the window grows automatically (the user's decision). Watch blocks count by their own height
  - It grows only when the birds actually can't be placed without overlapping at the current size, and only by as much as needed. Growing when they would fit leaves the birds sparse, which is a bug (the user's report)
  - "Can't be placed" is judged by the actual placement: the birds on screen stay where they are, and a bird or block that has no free spot among them overflows. How far is also found with the actual placement: the smallest size at which, with the birds on screen kept at the same place on screen, everything gets a spot without moving anyone (failing that, the smallest at which everything gets a spot). Until the window has grown, the bird that has no spot is placed overlapping others
  - It grows away from the display edges it sits near, decided per axis: a window in the right part of the display keeps its right edge and grows left, one in the bottom part keeps its bottom edge and grows up, and one in the middle grows both ways around its center (the user's decision). "Part" is the display's thirds, judged by the window's center (Claude's own decision)
  - It grows up to the visible area of the display the window is on (excluding the menu bar and the Dock). If the birds still don't fit at that size, birds that have no spot overlap others at the nearest place
  - Width and height grow together keeping the aspect ratio of the user's size (the user's decision)
  - A manual resize always wins, including making it smaller (the user's decision): the resized size becomes the user's size and the window is not grown back over it. It grows again only when a bird joins and no longer fits (Claude's own decision)
  - When birds leave, the window shrinks automatically (the user's request: 「今は自動でウィンドウ大きくなるが、自動で小さくもなるようにしてほしい」). It shrinks to the smallest size, never below the user's size, at which every bird and block still on screen keeps its place on screen, the same as on a drop, so no bird moves because another left (the user: 「キャラが消える時に、関係ないキャラも動いてしまってくずれることがある」). Before, it shrank to the smallest size at which they all got a spot and birds that would stick out moved to the nearest free spot inside (Claude's own decision), which moved birds the user hadn't touched. A shrink by hand still moves birds that would stick out to the nearest free spot inside, as above (the user: 「ちがう、手動でウィンドウをちいさくしたばあいはうごいていい」「おれがいってるのはじどうしゅくしょうな」)
    - It shrinks toward the same edges it grew away from (a window in the right part keeps its right edge, and so on), keeping the aspect ratio of the user's size (Claude's own decision)
    - It also shrinks when the user drops a bird or watch block, once per drop, by the same rule: the smallest size, never below the user's size, at which everything on screen keeps its place, so carrying a bird back in from the edge brings the window back (the user: 「この自動かくだいがはいれば、じどうしゅくしょうもいれればかんぺきやな」, choosing on drop over while dragging: 「どらっぐはなしたときやな」). Nothing moves for it: birds stay at the same place on screen
    - It shrinks only when birds leave or on a drop, not on other changes or during a drag, so the window doesn't keep resizing while the user watches (Claude's own decision)
  - The grown size is not saved; the next launch opens at the user's size
  - While the user drags a bird or watch block, the window also grows when the pointer comes within 24px of the garden's edge or goes past it, toward that edge only, so the bird can be carried further (the user: 「きゃらをどらっぐするのにあわせてウィンドウが自動でかくだいするようにもできる？」, approving with 「おk」). It keeps growing while the pointer stays there, up to the visible area of the display; the birds on screen stay at the same place on screen. Only the dragged axis grows, not keeping the aspect ratio of the user's size as the growth for fit does, since the user is pushing one way (Claude's own decision). The grown size is treated like any automatic growth: it is not the user's size, and the window shrinks back by the rule above when birds leave (Claude's own decision). If pointer events stop once the pointer leaves the window, the 24px band inside the edge alone drives it (Claude's own decision)
  - Only the floating window's Garden tab grows the window. Moving from the Garden to Perch, Recent activity, settings or the debug log in the floating window keeps the window's current size, so coming back to the Garden moves no bird (Claude's own decision, following the user: 「てかウィンドウを自動で縮めるときに鳥がうごくのがもんだいなのはわかってるか？」; before, the window went back to the user's size there and birds that had been outside it moved on return). The standard window stays at the user's size, and switching between the floating and standard window opens at the user's size as before
  - Every automatic shrink goes through one function that picks the smallest size, never below the user's size, at which every bird and block keeps its place, checked against the actual layout; when it can't be checked, the window doesn't shrink (the user: 「あと処理は共通化しろよ」). A page reload works out the current growth from the measured garden instead of taking it as the user's size
- Status lines: line 1 is the state and elapsed time, line 2 the tool name. Inside watch blocks, line 1 is the state and line 2 the elapsed time and tool name
- No event marks (✓ for done, "?" for waiting) under the birds in the Garden. Recent activity keeps its ✓ / ? on the left of each row, and a bird keeps its "?" and anger mark at its top right (the user: 「にわ、ではちぇっくまーくとかのあいこんをはずしたほうがすっきりするかなどう？」; Claude recommended dropping them, then the user: 「はずして。」)
- When the window is wide, Perch and Recent activity are centered at a max width of about 720px, and settings at 560px. The Garden uses the full width
